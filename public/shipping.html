Shipping & returns

Shipping, damage and returns

Last updated November 5, 2026

How an order works

You save a card when you commission a portrait, and nothing is charged. Within 48 hours (often within 24) we email you a proof. You can ask for changes as many times as you need at no cost. We charge your card only when you approve the proof, and then we print, frame and ship it.

If you decide not to go ahead before approving, just reply to your proof email or write to support@burinandco.com. You won't be charged anything.

Shipping

  • United States: free, by ground service. Most portraits arrive about two weeks after you approve the proof.
  • Outside the US: shipped at cost. We quote it before you pay.
  • Once your portrait ships, write to us any time and we'll send you its tracking.

Framed portraits are made and shipped by our professional print and framing partner in the United States.

Damage in transit or a defect

Each portrait is packed for shipping, but if yours arrives damaged or with a printing or framing defect, email support@burinandco.com within 14 days of delivery, with a photo of the piece and the box. We'll reprint and reframe it or refund you, at no cost to you, and tell you whether we need the damaged one back.

Lost packages

If tracking shows no progress for 10 business days, or the package is marked delivered but you don't have it, write to us. We'll open a claim with the carrier and send a replacement.

Returns

Every portrait is drawn from your own photograph and made to order after you approve it, so we can't accept returns for a change of mind once the proof is approved. That's why you see and approve the proof before you pay. If something isn't right with the finished piece, tell us and we'll make it right.

Refunds

Approved refunds go back to the card you paid with. Your bank usually shows them within 5 to 10 business days.

Questions

Write to support@burinandco.com. A person reads every message.
