The work

Every badge, wheel and window, exactly right.

Engraved builds every tone from fine parallel lines, the way currency and share certificates have been engraved for two centuries. Studio is drawn the way master car designers present a build: glossy marker color in your exact paint, crisp highlights and mirror chrome on clean white paper. Heritage paints it the way the great mid-century advertising artists did, with paint that looks a foot deep. Either way, the subject itself is drawn exactly as it is.

Questions

Will it match my exact car or boat?

Yes. We work from your photograph and check the proof detail by detail. If something is off, tell us and we redraw it.

Can I order it as a gift for a client?

Yes. Many orders come from dealers and brokers at closing. Put the client's name on the plaque, enter their address at checkout, and we include no pricing in the box.

What if I don't have a good photo?

Send what you have. A listing photo, a dealer photo, or a phone picture taken from the side all work.

How long does it take?

Proof in 48 hours. After you approve, about two weeks to your door in the US.

Do you ship outside the US?

Yes, at cost. We quote it before you pay.
