Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Burin & Co. is a brand of Tenfold Commerce LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you visit burinandco.com or commission a portrait, and what we do with it. Questions go to support@burinandco.com.

What we collect

  • Your order: your name, email, the recipient's name, shipping address and phone if you give one, the photograph you upload, your choices (style, size, frame, caption) and any notes or change requests.
  • Payment: your card is entered on Stripe's secure page. We never see or store the full card number. Stripe tells us the card brand, the last four digits and whether a payment went through.
  • Emails you send us at support@burinandco.com or in reply to our emails.
  • How you found us: the ad or link that brought you here (for example utm tags), the page you landed on, and an approximate location (city and state) from your IP address.
  • With your consent only: cookies from Meta (Facebook and Instagram ads), Google (Analytics and Google Ads) and Microsoft Clarity (how pages are used). See Cookies below.

How we use it

  • To draw your portrait, send you the proof, charge your card when you approve it, and print, frame and ship it.
  • To answer your questions and send emails about your order.
  • To understand which ads and pages work, and to improve the site.
  • To prevent fraud and meet legal and tax obligations.

We don't sell your personal information. We don't use your photograph or portrait in our marketing without asking you first.

Who we share it with

Only the services that help us run the business, and only what each one needs:

  • Stripe: payments.
  • Replicate: the image-generation service our drawing process runs on. It receives your photograph to produce the drawing.
  • FinerWorks: printing, framing and shipping. It receives the final artwork and the recipient's name and address.
  • Resend: sends and receives our email.
  • Netlify: hosts the site and stores order files.
  • Meta, Google and Microsoft: only if you accept cookies. When you accept and then complete a purchase, we also send Meta the purchase value and a scrambled (hashed) copy of your email so it can measure our ads. Meta can't read your email from it.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or with a buyer if the business is ever sold.

Cookies

The site works without any advertising or analytics cookies. If you click Accept on the cookie banner, we load:

  • Meta Pixel, so Meta can tell us which ads led to a visit or an order, and show our ads to people likely to be interested.
  • Google Analytics and Google Ads, so we can count visits and orders, see which pages and ads bring people here, and measure our Google ads. Google gets the page you're on, never the private link to your proof.
  • Microsoft Clarity, which records how visitors scroll and click so we can improve the pages. It masks what you type.

If you click Decline, none of them load. You can change your choice any time with at the bottom of every page. The site also keeps a few items in your browser's own storage (your cookie choice, and the ad link you arrived from) that are never shared with advertisers.

How long we keep it

We keep order records, including your photo and portrait, for as long as we need them to support your order and reorders, and as long as tax and accounting rules require. Ask us and we'll delete your photo and portrait files sooner.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop using it for advertising. Email support@burinandco.com. We'll answer within 30 days, and we may need to confirm it's you. Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have further rights under local law. We honor them, and we don't treat you differently for using them.

Children

The site is not meant for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted on the site.
